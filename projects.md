## Finance & Risk Modeling

### Multiscale Diffusion in Random Networks (Columbia) — *Risk & Uncertainty Quantification*
Built a scalable Monte Carlo PDE solver for random 3D Voronoi networks—techniques directly applicable to Monte Carlo-based Value at Risk (VaR) modeling and portfolio risk analysis.

**Highlights:**
- Implemented simulation workflows for stochastic network geometries (analogous to Monte Carlo methods in financial risk)
- Investigated convergence and representative volume effects (critical for variance reduction in VaR)
- Observed scaling consistent with connected path structure behavior ($L_{REV} \propto \rho^{-1/2}$)
- **Finance Application:** These computational methods translate directly to derivative pricing, risk aggregation, and stress testing

### Smectite-Clay Transport Modeling (ESTP / Ecole des Ponts / ADEME) — *Data Integration & Parameter Estimation*
Developed high-fidelity C++ and FreeFEM++ models and integrated experimental/simulation datasets for parameter estimation—techniques essential in financial data integration and model calibration.

**Highlights:**
- Built and validated computational models for an experimentally developed smectite-clay system
- Identified electrostatic interactions as the dominant rate-limiting transport mechanism
- Contributed to model-driven research direction in the IRGAK project
- **Finance Application:** Multi-source data integration, parameter estimation, and validation frameworks applicable to financial model calibration and backtesting

### Tsunami Wave Propagation Modeling (ESTP) — *Optimization & Performance Analysis*
Optimized large-scale MATLAB models to study tsunami mitigation from coral reefs and mangroves—demonstrating computational optimization techniques applicable to portfolio optimization.

**Highlights:**
- Reduced runtime by approximately 100x through model and implementation optimization (critical for high-frequency trading systems)
- Captured up to 40% run-up reduction induced by sustainable natural barriers
- Validated model behavior against experimental datasets
- **Finance Application:** Optimization methodology, performance tuning, and efficient computation frameworks applicable to algorithmic trading and portfolio rebalancing
